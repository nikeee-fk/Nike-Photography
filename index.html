About

Photographer / Visual Artist

I photograph the rhythm of places — how light settles, how air changes, and how time leaves traces in the landscape.

My interest is not only in scenery, but in the atmosphere that surrounds it: stillness, weather, silence, and the nearly invisible shift that happens between moments.

Mountains, coastlines, forests, and cities all become part of the same visual language.
